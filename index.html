Welcome!

Welcome to my research project. Over the course of a year, or around 52 weeks, I wil be researching and finding out more about various space related things to broaden my understanding of space- an interest which has been very dear to me since I was young.

I will research something that takes my interest that particular week. This includes man made items, like space ships and probes and rovers, investigating how they where made, the engineering behind them and what purpose they serve. I will also look into naturally occuirng things in space like planets, stars nebulae and more!

What will I research?

  • Stars and stellar evolution
  • Planets and exoplanets
  • Nebulae and galaxies
  • Spectroscopy
  • Spacecraft and probes
  • Other areas of space science
