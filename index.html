My year of Astronomy Research

Introduction

Welcome to my research project

Over the year, I will delegate a week to researching various astronomical objects, topics and related items- like stars, planets, exoplanets, satelites, spaceships and probes, nebulae and spectroscopy. Each week I will focus on one of these ideas in depth, discovering how it was made or discovered, what its composed of or how engineers built it, scientific methods used to study it and more.
