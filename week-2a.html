Introduction

Betlegeuse is the second brightest star in the constellation orion. Orion includes the asterism orions belt, which includes other bright stars. An asterism is a recognisable pattern of stars in the sky. It is 700 light years away from earth, and is apart of the winter hexagon- an asterism of stars in winetrs northern hemisphere. Inside the winter hexagon is thw inter triangle- a smaller asterism containing our star Betelgeuse, Procyon and Sirius. Star Type

Betelgeuse is a red supergiant. Red meaning it is cooler, just like Proxima Centauri, and supergiant telling us that it is far larger and luminous than giant stars. Betelgeuse is around 10 million years old- younger than our sun by quite a lot, yet it is brighter by around 14,000 times, and about 700 times the size of the sun. Despite all of this, its cooler than the sun since its a red supergiant, its surface temperature being around 3500 K.
