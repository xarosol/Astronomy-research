Introduction

Betlegeuse is the second brightest star in the constellation orion. Orion includes the asterism orions belt, which includes other bright stars. An asterism is a recognisable pattern of stars in the sky. It is 700 light years away from earth, and is apart of the winter hexagon- an asterism of stars in winetrs northern hemisphere. Inside the winter hexagon is thw inter triangle- a smaller asterism containing our star Betelgeuse, Procyon and Sirius.
