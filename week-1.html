Introduction

Proxima Centauri is a star in the constellation centaurus. It is the second closest star to earth, and is located about 4.2 light years away. (For reference, 1 light year is the distance light travels in a year- so about 9.4 trillion km. For only the 2nd closest star to earth, that is already crazy far!) Proxima Centauri is a red dwarf star, burning at 3100 K. Red Dwarf stars are the largest population of stars in the galaxy- but they're too dim to be seen with the naked eye. So if you try to look for our star Proxima Centauri, unfortunately, you wont be able to see it. However! Red dwarf star's limited radiance helps them lengthen their lifetimes. Proxima Centauri is predicted to stay in its current phase for over 4 trillion years. Far longer thaan our sun, which is predicted to die in about 4 billion years. Proxima Centauri orbits 2 other stars in its system- Alpha Centauri A and B. Its the dimmest of the stars, however, its brightness can flucuate every few minutes.
