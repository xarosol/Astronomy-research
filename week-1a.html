Introduction

Proxima Centauri is a star in the constellation centaurus. It is the second closest star to earth, and is located about 4.2 light years away. (For reference, 1 light year is the distance light travels in an earth year- so about 9.4 trillion km. For only the 2nd closest star to earth, that is already crazy far!) It is theorized to be orbited by 3 planets, Proxima Centauri b, c and d- the closest planets to us outside the solar system. None are habitable- with b being tidally locked and and likely unable to support an atmosphere, c being theorised to be -233C and d being outside the habitable zone. Proxima Centauri itself orbnits two other stars in the Centauri system- Alpha Centauri A (Rigil Kentaurus) and B (Toliman), taking 550,000 years to complete an orbit around both.

Star Type

Proxima Centauri is a red dwarf star, burning at 3100 K. Red Dwarf stars are the largest population of stars in the galaxy- but they're too dim to be seen with the naked eye. So if you try to look for Proxima Centauri, unfortunately, you usually wont be able to see it. However! Red dwarf star's limited radiance helps them lengthen their lifetimes. Proxima Centauri is predicted to stay in its current phase for over 4 trillion years. Far longer thaan our sun, which is predicted to die in about 4 billion years. Now, earlier I said that you usually cant see Proxima Centauri with the naked eye. This holds true- however it has been obsereved by scientists that Proxima Centauri is a flare star. This means that convection proccesses in its body make it randomly change brightness. Stellar flares are massive releases of electromagnetic radiation, from all over the EM spectrum. In its brightesr flare recorded, it became '14,000 times brighter when seen in UV wavelengths over the span of a few seconds,' said Meredith MacGregor, an assistant professor at the CASA (Centre for Astrophysics and Space Astronomy).

Composition, mass and age

Stellar Evolution models have been used to estimate Proxima Centauris mass and abundance of elements in it. Its mass is around M ≈ 0.12 M⊙. This means that its mass is around 0.12 the mass of the sun- the unit 'M⊙' having the value of 2 × 10^33g, called 'solar mass'. It is around 5 billion years old, and mostly composed of helium and hydrogen, as most red dwarf stars. However, there are trace amounts of heavier elements than helium also present- called metallicity (Z). The metallicity of Proxima Centauri is 0.004 < Z < 0.01, meaning that heavy elements make up more than 0.4% but less than 1% of its mass.
